# Just Sing

Songs, singing performances, and preparation for karaoke and spoken Korean practice.

## Language

**Prepared song**:
A recording with synchronized instrumental and vocal stems, timed lyrics, and a reference pitch track.
_Avoid_: Raw song when referring to a playable preparation.

**Practice section**:
A continuous portion of a prepared song selected for a singing performance, containing whole scored phrases.
_Avoid_: Audio trim when the source recording is unchanged.

**Lyric line**:
A timed unit of displayed lyrics, with original text and optional romanization and translation.

**Scored phrase**:
A timed singing unit associated with a lyric line, including its held ending, on which a singer receives a rating.
_Avoid_: Lyric line when referring specifically to scoring boundaries.

**Duet arrangement**:
An ordered assignment of scored phrases to Player 1 or Player 2 for one continuous performance.

**Listening review**:
A person's check of lyrics, timing, pitch, and singer transitions against the prepared recording.
_Avoid_: Automatic validation when claiming a song has been reviewed.
